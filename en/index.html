Our Hotel

A warm stop in Arsin

Dedehan Hotel is located in the Yeşiltepe neighbourhood of Arsin, Trabzon. Whether you are on a business trip, a family holiday or touring the Black Sea, we offer a calm stay away from the city crowds, yet close to everything.

The hotel has 25 air-conditioned rooms, a buffet breakfast every morning, a 24-hour front desk and room service. Markets, pharmacies and restaurants are within walking distance.

  • Buffet breakfast (08:00–11:00)
  • 24-hour front desk & room service
  • Free private parking
  • Airport shuttle (surcharge)
